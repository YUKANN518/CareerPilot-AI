# Hong Kong Job Search Guide

This synthetic guide is provided only for the CareerPilot demo knowledge base.

Candidates should compare the role description with evidence in their resume, confirm working-location expectations, and verify any language or eligibility requirement before applying. A strong application records the source of each claim and avoids assuming that a preferred qualification is mandatory.

When tracking an application, keep the date, current stage, next action and source link. Do not store identity documents, government identifiers or confidential employer material in the demo system.
